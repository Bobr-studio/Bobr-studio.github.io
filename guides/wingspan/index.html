How to score Wingspan the base game

At the end of the game, take each player's player board and cards, then count the score in 6 parts. The round goals need one extra check: first see which side of the goal board your game used.

When to count

  1. After the 4th round, count each player's score. You can total the round goals as you play, then check them again at the end.
  2. Use the same order for everyone: birds, bonus cards, round goals, eggs, food stored on bird cards, then cards tucked under birds.
  3. Keep leftover food tokens separate. They do not add points; they only break a tie in the final score.

Bird cards

Look at the points printed on each bird you played.

Add those printed values. The bird cards are in the rows of your player board; count only the birds you played.

Where to look: the point value on each bird card in your habitat.

If your played birds show 5, 8, 3 and 7 points, this line is 23 points.

Bonus cards

Read each bonus card you kept and score it using its own condition.

Check the birds or other items named by the card, count the ones that meet that condition, and use the card's scoring instruction. Add the results from your bonus cards together.

Where to look: your bonus cards and the birds or items they ask you to count.

If a bonus card gives 1 point for each qualifying bird and you have 4, that card adds 4 points.

Bonus cards do not all score the same way, so check the condition on every card rather than treating them as printed bird points.

Round goals

Check the side of the goal board first.

The blue side scores your own count: each qualifying item is 1 point, up to 5 points in a round. The green side compares all players' counts and awards points by place. Count each round separately, then add your 4 round scores.

Where to look: the goal tiles and the relevant birds, eggs, nests or other items on each player's board, as the tile describes.

Green side examples

For green goals, only players with at least 1 qualifying item take a place. Tied players share the points for the places they occupy, rounded down; the next place is skipped.

Round 1: tie for 1st

Counts 3, 3 and 1. First and second place are worth 4 and 1, so the tied players each get 2. The player with 1 item is 3rd and gets 0.

Round 4: tie for 2nd

Counts 5, 2 and 2. The leader gets 7. The tie shares 4 and 3, so each tied player gets 3.

A zero is no place

In round 2, counts 2 and 0 score 5 and 0. The player with none does not take 2nd place.

On the blue side, 7 items score 5 points, not 7. On the green side, a tie changes the points for every tied player and skips the next place.

Eggs

Count every egg on your bird cards.

Each egg is worth 1 point. Check all habitat rows so eggs on any bird are included.

Where to look: egg tokens sitting on your bird cards.

12 eggs add 12 points.

Food stored on birds

Count food tokens tucked onto bird cards as stored food.

Each food token stored on a bird is worth 1 point. Count those tokens once; food still in your supply is not part of this scoring line.

Where to look: food tokens on bird cards that store food.

3 stored food tokens add 3 points.

Cards tucked under birds

Count the cards tucked beneath your bird cards.

Each tucked card is worth 1 point, whatever points are printed on the tucked card. Do not add the bird's printed value again.

Where to look: cards partly tucked under your played birds.

5 tucked cards add 5 points.

Common mistakes

  • On the blue goal side, stop at 5 points per round even if you count more than 5 items.
  • On the green side, leave players with 0 items unranked, and split tied places by rounding down.
  • Count eggs, stored food and tucked cards as separate scoring lines.
  • Leftover food in your supply is only a tie-breaker; it does not add to the score.
  • Count tucked cards as 1 point each, not by the points printed on those cards.

Who wins?

Add the 6 scoring lines. The player with the most points wins. If players tie, compare their leftover food tokens; the tied player with more food wins. If they still tie, they share the win.

A whole game, counted

Suppose your player board and cards give you 42 bird points and 9 bonus-card points. Your round goals score 2, 5, 3 and 4; you have 12 eggs, 3 stored food tokens and 5 tucked cards.

  1. Bird cards add 42 points.
  2. Bonus cards add 9 points.
  3. The 4 round goals add 14 points: 2 + 5 + 3 + 4.
  4. Your 12 eggs add 12 points.
  5. Your 3 stored food tokens add 3 points.
  6. Your 5 tucked cards add 5 points.

42 + 9 + 14 + 12 + 3 + 5 = 85 points.

If another player also scores 85, compare leftover food. For example, 3 food beats 1 food.

Questions players ask

Does leftover food score points?

No. Keep it aside for resolving a tie on total points.

What happens when players tie on a green goal?

Add the points for the places they share and divide evenly, rounding down. The next place is skipped.

Do tucked cards score their printed points?

No. Each card tucked under a bird is worth 1 point for this end-game count.

Does this cover expansions?

This guide covers the base game; expansions can add different scoring rules.
