Dots RPG — Privacy Policy

Developer: Bobr Studio · Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Dots RPG (the “Game”) handles user information.

In short

The Game does not collect personal data such as your name, e-mail, contacts or location. With your permission it sends anonymous statistics and crash reports so we can improve the Game and fix bugs. Without an internet connection nothing is sent and the Game works as usual.

What is stored on your device

  • settings (language, sound, vibration, on-board hints, your statistics choice) and player names, if you entered them;
  • an unfinished game, so you can continue it later;
  • statistics of games against the computer opponent (wins, losses, draws).

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it: reset statistics in settings, clear the app data in Android settings, or uninstall the Game.

What may be sent

We use Google's Firebase service (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics).

  • Anonymous statistics — only if you allowed it on first launch or in settings: which modes, boards and maps are chosen, how long a game lasts and how it ends.
  • Crash reports — on by default, can be turned off in settings: a technical description of the error, device model, Android and Game version.
  • A random identifier of this Game installation is sent along with them. It is not linked to your name, account or advertising ID.

Data is encrypted in transit, used only to improve the Game, never sold and never shared with third parties for advertising. Google keeps it for a limited time under its own rules: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What the Game does not do

  • no registration; it never asks for your name, e-mail or other contact details;
  • no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or files;
  • no advertising ID and no ads;
  • no in-app purchases.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If future versions add features involving data (for example, ads), we will update this page before such a version is released and change the effective date.
