Score Night — Privacy Policy

Developer: Bobr Studio · Effective October 12, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile app Score Night (the “App”), a score calculator for board games, handles user information.

In short

We do not collect personal data: no registration, no e-mail, contacts or files. Everything you enter — players’ names and scores — stays on your device. The App does not send anything to us.

What is stored on your device

  • games you have played or started: the game, date, player names and colours you entered, and the scores;
  • settings: language, vibration, keeping the screen on, hints, and which game introductions you have already seen.

This data is not sent to us. You can delete it by deleting games in the App’s history, clearing the app data in Android settings, or uninstalling the App. If Android backup is turned on for your device, the system may include this data in your device backup to your Google account; we have no access to it.

Permissions

  • vibration — a short vibration when you tap buttons; it can be turned off in the App’s settings;
  • keeping the screen on during a game so the phone does not go to sleep while you count; it can be turned off in the settings.

What the App does not do

  • no account and no registration;
  • no access to location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts or files;
  • we do not sell data and do not use analytics services.
  • the App does not connect to the internet; links to our website, e-mail and Google Play open in other apps on your phone.

Unofficial app

Score Night is an independent helper for counting points. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the publishers of the games it supports. Game names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify the games.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If future versions add features involving data (for example, ads or purchases), we will update this page before such a version is released and change the effective date.
