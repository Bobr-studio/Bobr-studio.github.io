Score Night — Privacy Policy

Developer: Bobr Studio · Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile app Score Night (the “App”), a score calculator for board games, handles user information.

In short

We do not collect personal data: no registration, no e-mail, contacts or files. Everything you enter — players’ names and scores — stays on your device. The App does not send anything to us. The free version shows ads provided by Unity Ads / Yandex Mobile Ads; the ad network may process technical data about your device as described below. Each game can be tried for free; after that you can unlock it with a one-time purchase or, if you choose, by watching a video ad that opens it for 24 hours. Any purchase removes ads.

What is stored on your device

  • games you have played or started: the game, date, player names and colours you entered, and the scores;
  • settings: language, vibration, keeping the screen on, hints, and which game introductions you have already seen;
  • which games you have bought, how many free games of each game you have finished, and until when a game is unlocked after watching an ad.

This data is not sent to us. You can delete it by deleting games in the App’s history, clearing the app data in Android settings, or uninstalling the App. If Android backup is turned on for your device, the system may include this data in your device backup to your Google account; we have no access to it.

Permissions

  • vibration — a short vibration when you tap buttons; it can be turned off in the App’s settings;
  • keeping the screen on during a game so the phone does not go to sleep while you count; it can be turned off in the settings.

Advertising (free version)

Ads are shown in the game list and in the history — never while you are entering scores. They are served by Unity Ads SDK (Unity Technologies Inc.) / Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (YANDEX LLC and its affiliates). To show and measure ads, the SDK may collect: the device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), device model and OS version, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and information about ad views and clicks. The SDK includes the AppMetrica component (also by Yandex), which collects technical diagnostics such as crash reports. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown only non-personalized ads.

The App mainly uses Unity Ads; some versions may use the Yandex SDK instead. Unity Ads may additionally collect other device identifiers, information about in-app purchases and technical diagnostics, and also uses the data to prevent fraud. Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Yandex privacy policy: yandex.com/legal/confidential; Yandex Mobile Ads terms: yandex.com/legal/mobileads_sdk_agreement.

Purchases

Games (one at a time or all games at once) are sold through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we never receive your card or payment details — only a confirmation that the purchase was made. See the Google Privacy Policy.

What the App does not do

  • no account and no registration;
  • no access to location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts or files;
  • we do not sell your data.
  • apart from ads (see above), the App does not send data over the internet; links to our website, e-mail and Google Play open in other apps on your phone.

Unofficial app

Score Night is an independent helper for counting points. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the publishers of the games it supports. Game names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify the games.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If future versions add features involving data (for example, ads or purchases), we will update this page before such a version is released and change the effective date.
