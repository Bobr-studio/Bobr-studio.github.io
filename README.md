<p align="center">

Bobr Studio is a tiny indie studio. Like beavers, we build slowly and carefully —
cozy strategy games that are hard to put down and handy apps that just work.
Everything we make is for players around the world, in their own language.

## Our projects

| | |
|---|---|
| **[Dots RPG](https://bobrstudio.dev/dots-rpg/)** | Surround your rival's dots — Classic from the school notebook and Adventure with gold, forests and rivers. |
| **[Chrono Blocks](https://bobrstudio.dev/chrono-blocks/)** | Split talks, meetings and workouts into timed blocks and always keep your pace. |
| **[Score Night](https://bobrstudio.dev/score-night/)** | Enter what's on the table — Score Night does the board game scoring for you. |
